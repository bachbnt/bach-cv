# Travel skin (id `travel`, CSS prefix `tl-`)

State: built on the branch `feat/travel-skin` (rebased on the `dev` that had Trade at `a48f97e`, so it still lacks the later polish of
Trade: the zoom fix, motion scale, `@layer base` focus ring, ink colours, `useNow` and the other shared hooks). Its README
(`src/skins/travel/README.md` on that branch) is the as-built description. Mock: `design/skins/travel*.html` (local, may be absent);
the owner liked its style, fonts and colours, which stay.

## Metaphor

A **2.5D trip map of Nha Trang**. The stations are the real roles of the career; the map is the real city drawn in an isometric view
with water, boats, clouds and birds.

| Page         | Metaphor                        | What the data became                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home         | the trip map and its overview   | the whole route; the current role is the destination ("You are here") |
| Resume       | the route, every role a station | roles and the school are stops; responsibilities are "things to see"  |
| Projects     | postcards from the sights built | projects are photo postcards with their screenshots                   |
| Certificates | passport stamps                 | one stamp per certificate                                             |
| Services     | tours and bookings              | services are tours; the contact form is the booking form              |
| Payment      | tickets and the payment desk    | each account is a ticket; the QR is the payment desk                  |
| About        | the traveller's passport        | profile, core stack as the packing list, education as the trailhead   |

The page is a card over the map (a bottom sheet on a phone); the map keeps its camera and the traveller when the page changes.

## Decisions that matter

- **Real geography, accurate to the place.** `map/geo.ts` is GENERATED by `scripts/travel/build-geo.mjs` from OpenStreetMap (coast,
  islands, rivers, roads, beaches, forests, built-up areas, peaks) with ONE equirectangular projection (`map/projection.ts` is the same
  at run time); the skin never fetches a map; keep `geo.ts` under 40 KB. Credit "Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" in the
  README, the UI (a line on the map) and the repo README. The owner rejected a first map that was far from the real one: unit tests pin
  the directions (Hon Chong north of the centre, Long Son and the cathedral west, Vinpearl north-east across the sea, islands south-east,
  Cam Ranh south, sights on land, boats at sea).
- **18 famous sights** with real coordinates (Po Nagar, Hon Chong, Long Son pagoda, the cathedral, Tram Huong tower, Dam Market, the
  Yersin museum, the Institute of Oceanography, Bao Dai villa, Thap Ba mud baths, Vinpearl, the cable car, the islands, Monkey Island,
  Ba Ho waterfalls). They are SCENERY: `aria-hidden`, never mixed with the owner's data; each is an original drawing in `map/sprites/`.
- **Stops are real roles** from `routeStops()` (`lib/derive/route-stops.ts`: the school first, jobs by start month, a part-time job
  overlapping a full-time one is a side trip) and `placeStops` puts them along the real coast road from south to north; the traveller
  (owner's photo in a pin) rides it with the Web Animations API.
- Camera: drag, wheel, pinch, arrows, `+` / `-`, buttons for zoom and "show the whole trip"; the page and the open stop decide where it
  flies. Animations pause off screen, with the tab hidden and with reduced motion.
- Every page is a real link in the rail (icon bar on a phone); the map is never the only way to anything.

## Known rough edges (as of the branch)

Small island sprites look like green mounds; the Bao Dai villa crowds nearby pins at far zoom; mountains barely visible; night land and
sea colours are close; the night market of the mock was replaced by Cho Dam (not in OSM); `credentialUrl` not shown; screenshots were
checked with mock (purple) images only; the CSS is ~1150 lines (`map.css` 575), more than Dev or Trade (~470): move what utilities can
say into the TSX in the next pass.

## Not built on purpose

The mock's timeline scrubber (the itinerary and "Start the trip" cover it with real controls); numbers Standard does not have
(developers led, Android modules); a moving technologies strip (a still row of luggage tags instead).
