# The Travel skin

The site as a **2.5D trip map of Nha Trang**. The stations of the trip are the real roles of the career; the map is the real city
(OpenStreetMap), drawn in an isometric view with water, boats, clouds and birds. Same information as Standard, its own interface.
Id `travel`, class prefix `tl-`, theme `src/app/tailwind-theme-travel.css`, palette and base rules `travel.css`, other CSS in `css/`.

## Pages are parts of the trip

| Page         | Metaphor                        | What the data became                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home         | the trip map and its overview   | the whole route, the current role is the destination ("You are here") |
| Resume       | the route, every role a station | roles and the school are stops; responsibilities are "things to see"  |
| Projects     | postcards from the sights built | projects are photo postcards with their screenshots                   |
| Certificates | passport stamps                 | one stamp per certificate                                             |
| Services     | tours and bookings              | services are tours; the contact form is the booking form              |
| Payment      | tickets and the payment desk    | each account is a ticket; the QR is the payment desk                  |
| About        | the traveller's passport        | profile, core stack as the packing list, the trailhead (education)    |

The page is a card over the map (a sheet that rises from the bottom on a phone); the map keeps its camera and the traveller when the page
changes. Every page is a real link in the rail (a bar of icons on a phone): the map is never the only way to anything.

## The map

- **Real geography.** `map/geo.ts` is GENERATED by `scripts/travel/build-geo.mjs` from OpenStreetMap (coast, islands, forests,
  built-up areas, the Cai River and other water, beaches, roads, peaks). The script projects with ONE equirectangular projection
  (`cos(lat)` correction, 20000 units per degree of latitude), simplifies (Douglas-Peucker), clips to the map box and writes compact
  SVG paths; the skin never fetches a map. `map/projection.ts` is the same projection at run time (`project`, `unproject`).
  Credit: "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" in this file, in the UI (`travel-map.ts` copy, a line on the map) and in the
  README of the repo.
- **Regenerate** (queries run against the Overpass API, bounding box `12.14,109.10,12.44,109.40`; out geom):
  `natural=coastline` ways; `waterway` rivers/streams with a name, `natural=water` ways and relations; `highway` motorway to secondary
  (all of the box) and tertiary/residential/unclassified in the city box `12.19,109.165,12.285,109.215`; `natural=beach`;
  `landuse` residential/commercial/retail/industrial; `natural=wood|scrub` and `landuse=forest`; `natural=peak`. Save each answer as
  `<name>.json` (`coast river roads streets beach landuse wood peaks`) in a folder and run
  `node scripts/travel/build-geo.mjs <folder>`.
- **View.** `map/iso.ts`: the flat map is turned 40 degrees clockwise (north points up and to the right, the sea is at the lower
  right) and squashed (0.55). The ground is SVG (`layers/ground.tsx`: sea and slab with broad slanting bands of light and a few bands of
  shallow water, then land with its earth edge), a layer of sparse crests moved by `transform` only (`layers/waves.tsx`), the foam along the
  shore as a layer of its own that breathes by opacity (`layers/shore.tsx`), the city SVG polygons (`layers/city.tsx`, houses seeded along
  the real streets), sprites HTML. The city is thinned so the sights stand out: tall houses are squashed (`map/city-shape.ts`), no house
  stands on a road or a river (`map/segments.ts`) or on the ground around a sight.
- **Sights** (`map/sights.ts`, copy `content/copy/travel-map.ts`): 23 famous places with real coordinates (Po Nagar towers, Hon
  Chong, Long Son pagoda, the cathedral, Tram Huong tower, Dam Market, the Yersin museum, the Institute of Oceanography, Bao Dai villa,
  the Thap Ba mud baths, Vinpearl on Hon Tre, the cable car, Hon Mieu, Hon Tam, Hon Mot, Hon Mun, Monkey Island, Ba Ho waterfalls, the Do Theatre, Co Tien mountain, the Truc Lam Phung Thuy Son monastery, Dong Bo mountain and Hon Ro hill) and
  signs for places off the map (Cam Ranh airport, Bai Dai, Doc Let). They are SCENERY: `aria-hidden`, never mixed with the owner's
  data. Each has an original drawing in `map/sprites/` (about 100 shapes at most, no filter, mask or animation of its own: the page holds about
  4,800 elements). A sight on a peak (`onPeak`) takes the place of the plain mountain there. The name tags are a layer of their own over
  every drawing (`data-label`), and `map/use-declutter.ts` (with the pure `map/declutter.ts`) runs once the camera rests: a tag that a card
  of a stop would cover moves to another side of its sight (below, left, right, above) and is left out only when none is free; each card
  of a stop takes the side that covers the drawings less.
- **Stations.** The stops are `routeStops()` of `lib/derive/route-stops.ts` (school first, then jobs by start month; a part-time job that
  overlaps a full-time job is a side trip). The rule that gives them a place (`map/route.ts`, `placeStops`): the stops on the main
  road take the places of `MAIN_PLACES` along the real coast road (Tran Phu street, then Pham Van Dong road, from the OSM road graph), in
  the order of the career: two in the empty south, one across the Cai River north of Hon Chong, and the current one at the end of the road,
  beside the Do Theatre. They are stretches with no sight beside them (a test keeps them clear), so the cards and the names do not cover
  each other. A side trip takes a place of its own (the sandbank of the river beside Po Nagar, in the houses) and joins the road where it
  is nearest; one more takes a spur toward the hills. With more stops than places they are spaced evenly. The road goes on past the end
  of the OSM coast road to the Do Theatre (`NORTH_ROAD`, the next points of Pham Van Dong road). The card of a stop (its name and period)
  shows when its pin is pointed at or focused, or for the stop that is open; the pin keeps its accessible name. The traveller (the
  owner's photo in a pin) rides along the same road with the Web Animations API.
- **Camera.** Drag, wheel, pinch, arrow keys, `+` and `-`; buttons for zoom and "show the whole trip"; the page and the open stop decide
  where it flies (`config.ts`, `PAGE_FOCUS`). Animations pause when the map is off screen, the tab is hidden or the visitor asked for
  less motion.

## The frame and the windows

- **Header.** The owner's logo is a stamp on the bar, Download CV is the one coral call to action, and the bottom edge of the bar is a dashed
  road (`tl-road`) like the route on the map. Order of the menus is still Language, Skin, Theme.
- **BB Bot** (`shell/overlay-chat-*.tsx`): the travel guide as the road of the conversation. The head has the contour lines of a map and the
  logo as a pin; in the log a dashed route runs down the left, the guide's answers are stops on it, the visitor's questions are luggage tags
  with a punched hole, the wait is a dot walking a few dashes (`tl-guide-walk`, the only loop of the card, it runs only during the wait), the
  questions not asked yet are tags to pick, the send button is a stamp, and the limit of messages is a stretch of road that fills up. The launcher
  is a map pin. The session, limits, errors and Try again are the Standard ones (`useChatSession`).
- **Connect** is a postcard (airmail edge, the owner's photo as the stamp, the email as the address) and every link is a stamp with a
  dashed edge, grouped as in Standard.
- **About** is put together as you watch (`tl-portrait-in`, `tl-chip-in`, `tl-photo-in`): the portrait is stamped on, the specialties pop in one by
  one and the photos drop on the table. Only transform properties and opacity move, and reduced motion shows the final page.

## Rules of the skin

- Words only this skin has: `content/copy/travel*.ts`. Every control that looks different keeps the Standard accessible name.
- Colours are tokens (`bg-tl-panel`, `fill-tl-land-1`, `var(--tl-coral)`); no hex in components. Hover only inside `@media (hover: hover)`.
- Derived data (stops, years, skill use, groups) comes from `src/lib/derive/`; the map's geometry and layout maths stay here.

## Tests

- Unit (`tests/unit/skin/travel-*.test.ts`, `tests/unit/lib/derive/route-stops.test.ts`): the projection and the orientation of the sights and stations
  (Hon Chong north of the centre, Long Son and the cathedral west, Vinpearl north-east across the sea, the islands south-east, Cam Ranh south, the
  sights on land, the boats at sea, the city beach with water to its east), the coast road and `placeStops`, the camera, the size of `geo.ts`
  (under 40 KB), the CSS rules (prefix, hover only in `@media (hover: hover)`, palette in both themes) and the copy keys in both languages.
- Playwright (`tests/automation/skins/travel-*.spec.ts`): the frame and menus, Home, Resume, About, the map (controls, keys, drag, pins), the data
  states and the screens that are not a page, and for each other page its own spec (Projects, Certificates, Services, Payment, overlays).

## Not built on purpose

- The mock's timeline scrubber (a track of the years with a handle): the itinerary and "Start the trip" cover the same ground with real controls.
- Numbers of the mock that Standard does not have (developers led, Android modules): Home shows only what is derived from the data (years in
  software with `careerYears`, the number of stops).
- The moving technologies strip is a still row of luggage tags (nothing moves, so there is no pause button to keep).
